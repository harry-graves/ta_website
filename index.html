About me

Hello, I'm Christine

I'm both a qualified and practising nurse, and I live and work in Sutton Coldfield. I set up my aesthetics practice 13 years ago, working from the comfort of my own home.

I currently carry out wrinkle-relaxing injections and use dermal fillers from Q-Med such as Restylane, Perlane and Restylane Lipp. I also offer Glycolic skin peels using GLYDERM to give you beautiful, healthy-looking skin.

Previously I worked for the Harley Medical Group in Edgbaston, where I trained as a nurse therapist carrying out procedures ranging from laser hair removal to injecting dermal fillers for lines and wrinkles, as well as counselling for cosmetic procedures.

I hold full medical malpractice insurance, giving you peace of mind not offered by many other practitioners, along with the certification, accreditation and qualifications required to write your prescriptions.

What I do

Treatments

Three treatments, each tailored to you and discussed fully at your consultation.

Anti-Wrinkle

Several small injections temporarily relax the muscles that cause lines – the crow's feet around the eyes, the frown lines between the eyebrows, or the forehead. The face looks more relaxed and smooth, and results usually last 3–4 months.

  • Crow's feet
  • Between the eyebrows
  • Forehead lines

Dermal Fillers

Fillers reduce lines, folds and wrinkles, create fuller lips and enhance facial volume. The injectable gel is stabilised, non-animal hyaluronic acid, which closely resembles the hyaluronic acid found naturally in the body. Long lasting, but not permanent.

  • Nose-to-mouth lines
  • Lip enhancement
  • Cheeks and jawline

Skin Peels

Neostrata peels use alpha hydroxy acids (fruit acids) with excellent exfoliating, cell-stimulating and clarifying properties. They loosen and dislodge dead skin cells and hydrate the deeper layers, helping to reduce the signs of ageing, photo-damage, pigmentation and acne.

  • Ageing and photo-damage
  • Pigmentation
  • Acne
